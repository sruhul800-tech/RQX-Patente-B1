# RQX World Academy V15.4
Based on V15.3. Includes 30 ordered driving lessons across connected city grids and four extra genuinely driveable curves: coastal road, mountain hairpins, southern bend and northern forest route.

The embedded 2023 archive contains **7,139 questions in 25 topics**. Each road checkpoint asks a relevant study-topic question drawn from the original archive, with Vero/Falso and learning guidance. Theory-only subjects (licensing, insurance, medical fitness) appear as lesson checkpoints rather than depicted traffic signs.

**Limits:** shared map reused across several missions, procedural 3D graphics rather than photorealism, flat roads without elevation, educational simulated driving rules, not an official examination.

Backup: `backup/v15-3-before-world-roads`.
The app is a debug build; Android WebView hardware/browser support and mobile performance require device testing.
Do not add API keys, signing credentials or personal data to this public repository.
