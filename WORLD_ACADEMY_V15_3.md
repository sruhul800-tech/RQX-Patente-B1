# RQX World Academy V15.3

- Six district-based driving levels with GPS waypoints, native Android voice and persistent next-level unlock.
- Context-aware STOP, speed, pedestrian, roundabout and semaforo training.
- 7,139 historical 2023 questions from 25 topics are available in the driving game's quiz archive; figure questions attempt to load remote images.
- Level pass criterion: finish all checkpoints, answer at least three questions, at least 50% correct.
- 2D whole-city atlas shows 6 district markers; actual 3D areas belong to the existing connected V15 city (not six separate open-world assets).
- 2023 dataset is not official current Italian theory rules. 3D is stylized, and the AI Tutor still requires a separately configured secure backend.
- `backup/v15-2-1-before-levels` preserves the previous Android source.
